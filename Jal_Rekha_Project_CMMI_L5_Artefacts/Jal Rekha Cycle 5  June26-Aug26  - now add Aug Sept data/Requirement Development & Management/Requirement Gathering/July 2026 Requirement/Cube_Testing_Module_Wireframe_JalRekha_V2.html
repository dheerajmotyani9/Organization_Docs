Business Rules (BR)

11 rules
IDRuleDescriptionApplies to
BR‑CT‑01Cube image mandatory — Pass 1Geo‑tagged cube photo upload (one per cube ID) is mandatory at Step ① and Step ⑤ during Pass 1 (loop_iteration = 1). Submission is blocked without required photos.Screens 1, 5
BR‑CT‑02Cube image optional — Reinspection LoopPhoto upload is optional at Step ① and Step ⑤ during the Reinspection Loop / Pass 2 (loop_iteration = 2). Submission is not blocked by missing photos.Screens 1, 5
BR‑CT‑03Same RFI ID retainedThe Reinspection Loop reuses the original RFI ID / Pour Card — no new RFI is raised when restarting at Step ①.Master sequence
BR‑CT‑04Loop capped at 2 — no Pass 3loop_iteration is capped at 2 for Cube Testing. Step ⑥'s reject branch does not fire a third time; the workflow engine force‑closes the RFI after the Pass‑2 GM Action.Screens 6, 7, 8
BR‑CT‑05Rejection remark mandatoryGM PIU must enter a remark whenever rejecting at Step ⑥, in both Pass 1 and Pass 2.Screen 6
BR‑CT‑06FE change visibilityA newly assigned FE at Step ④ (replacing one who left the project) must be able to view the prior pass's Survey Detail, cube images and GM remarks as read‑only reference.Screen 4, FE‑change scenario
BR‑CT‑07Terminal final decisionGM PIU's Step ⑥ decision after the Reinspection Loop is terminal: Accept closes the RFI as Completed; Reject closes it as Rejected‑Escalated for offline resolution.Screens 6, 7
BR‑CT‑08Reject always routes to ContractorAt Step ⑥, the Reject branch always routes back to the Contractor (Step ①) — never to GM, SQC/TPIA or FE directly — restarting the full 6‑step sequence.Master sequence, Screen 6
BR‑CT‑097‑day / 28‑day dual scheduleEvery cube set runs two independent inspection checkpoints (7‑day and 28‑day compressive strength), each following the same 6‑step sequence.Screens 5, 6
BR‑CT‑10SQC/TPIA remark defaultingThe Step ③ SQC/TPIA recommendation text is offered as the default/pre‑filled GM Remark at Step ⑥, editable before submission.Screens 3, 6
BR‑CT‑11Uniform sequence, both passesSteps ①–⑥ (Contractor → GM Forward → SQC/TPIA Verify → FE Assign → FE Submit → GM Action) apply identically in Pass 1 and the Reinspection Loop; only the image‑mandatory flag and loop‑termination behaviour differ between passes.Master sequence
