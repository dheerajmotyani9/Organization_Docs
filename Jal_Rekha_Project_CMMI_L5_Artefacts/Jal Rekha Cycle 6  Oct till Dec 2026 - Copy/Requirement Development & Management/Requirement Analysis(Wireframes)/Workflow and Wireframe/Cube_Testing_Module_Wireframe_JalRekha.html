Business Rules (BR)

10 rules
IDRuleDescriptionApplies to
BR‑CT‑01Cube image mandatory — Cycle 1Geo‑tagged cube photo upload (one per cube ID) is mandatory before Contractor/FE can submit in Cycle 1 (loop_iteration = 1). Submission is blocked without required photos.Screens 1, 4
BR‑CT‑02Cube image optional — Cycle 2Photo upload is optional for the re‑inspection/re‑test cycle (loop_iteration = 2). Submission is not blocked by missing photos.Screen 9, Cycle 2 flow
BR‑CT‑03Same RFI ID retainedCycle 2 re‑inspection reuses the original RFI ID / Pour Card — no new RFI is raised for a re‑test cycle.Cycle 2 flow
BR‑CT‑04Loop capped at 2 — no repeatloop_iteration is capped at 2 for Cube Testing. The rejection branch cannot be re‑triggered a second time; the workflow engine force‑closes the RFI after the Cycle‑2 GM PIU decision.Screens 10, 11, GM Final node
BR‑CT‑05Rejection remark mandatoryGM PIU must enter a remark whenever rejecting a 7‑day or 28‑day cube result, in both Cycle 1 and Cycle 2.Screens 5, GM Reject node
BR‑CT‑06FE change visibilityA newly assigned FE (replacing one who left the project) must be able to view Cycle‑1 Survey Detail, cube images, GM's rejection remark as read‑only reference.Screen 7, FE‑change scenario
BR‑CT‑07Terminal final decisionGM PIU's decision after Cycle 2 is terminal: Accept closes the RFI; Reject closes the RFI as Rejected‑Escalated for offline resolution. Neither outcome re‑opens the loop.Screen 10, GM Final node
BR‑CT‑08No new RFI for corrective actionContractor's corrective‑action confirmation (re‑cast / correction) is logged against the existing RFI/Pour Card and does not require raising a fresh request.Screen 6
BR‑CT‑097‑day / 28‑day dual scheduleEvery cube set runs two independent inspection checkpoints (7‑day and 28‑day compressive strength). Each checkpoint has its own FE Survey Review/Report and GM Remark fields.Screens 3, 4, 5
BR‑CT‑10SQC/TPIA remark defaultingThe SQC/TPIA recommendation text is offered as the default/pre‑filled GM Remark on the final decision screen, editable before submission.Screens 5, 8, 10
