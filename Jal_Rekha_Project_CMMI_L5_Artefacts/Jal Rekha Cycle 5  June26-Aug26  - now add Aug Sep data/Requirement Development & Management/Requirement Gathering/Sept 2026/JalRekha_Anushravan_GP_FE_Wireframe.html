Business Requirements · Functional Requirements · Change Requests
GP Panchayat & FE Sub Engineer Module — Jal Rekha / Anusharavan (SVS)
Business Requirements (BR)
BR-01 GP Panchayat Role — Web & Mobile Access ▶
GP Panchayat sarpanch/secretary shall have a dedicated login on both web portal and mobile app. GP can create inspection lists (up to 8 questions), view FE survey results, and accept/reject work. All inspection reports shall be visible as a village-wise web report. Target go-live: 01 Sep 2026.
BR-02 FE Sub Engineer Role — Mobile Survey ▶
Field Engineers (Sub Engineers) shall receive inspection assignments on mobile. FE shall fill 6 questions (assigned by GP from a set of 8), capture up to 5 geotagged photos (offline GPS supported), and submit the survey. FE login is created by GM/Janpad Admin.
BR-03 Janpad Admin (GM) — FE & GP Mapping ▶
GM/Janpad creates FE Sub Engineer IDs and maps FE to one or more GPs and villages. GM assigns incoming GP inspection lists to appropriate FE. GM creates Janpad Sub Engineer ID (web version).
BR-04 Village All Inspection — Web Report ▶
A consolidated village-wise inspection report shall be generated on the web portal (Anusharavan GP — SVS). Reports shall be filterable by village, work type, FE, date range, and status. Export to Excel/PDF required. Go-live: 01 Sep 2026.
