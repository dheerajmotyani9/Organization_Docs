Defects Observed During UAT Walkthrough CR-04

Raised against the build shown on 17-Jul-2026, screenshots from both the Upload Activity form and the Abstract of RA Bill screen. These are treated as defects (not new scope) except where marked as a genuine new enhancement.

DEF-01 · Upload Activity
District / Block / GP / Village dropdowns render empty
Only the placeholder option ("-- Select District --" etc.) is shown; no master data loads into any of the four location dropdowns, so a user cannot progress past District. See screenshot 1.
DEF-02 · Upload Activity
Save button does not persist the record
Clicking Save gives no confirmation and the entry does not subsequently appear on the Sahbhagita Activity Report or Approval screens.
DEF-03 · Abstract of RA Bill
Filter-panel dropdowns render empty
Category, District, Block, Gram Panchayat and Village all show placeholder-only options with no selectable records, so the report cannot be filtered. See screenshot 2.
DEF-04 · Abstract of RA Bill
Category-driven location selection is too flat
Today Category alone toggles Gram Panchayat/Village visibility, but the fields don't cascade — GP and Village don't populate from the District/Block already chosen, and the Activity list isn't narrowed to the selected Village. The corrected flow: Category = Village Wise → Gram Panchayat populates from District/Block → Village populates from Gram Panchayat → Activity list is filtered to activities specific to that Village.

New enhancement confirmed alongside the fixes

Not a defect
ENH-01 — On the Abstract of RA Bill summary table, the "No. of Activities" column value itself becomes a hyperlink that opens the RA Bill Details drill-down (in addition to / instead of the whole row being clickable). Confirmed as a new enhancement, not a fix to existing behaviour.

ENH-02 — RA Bill No. and Activity on the Abstract of RA Bill filter panel are confirmed as multi-select controls (selecting more than one RA Bill and/or more than one Activity at a time), restoring the As-Is multi-select behaviour rather than replacing it with the single-select proposed earlier in this BRD.
Upload Activity — To‑Be CR-01 CR-04

Click through the fields. District and Block are always populated from live master data. Category determines whether Gram Panchayat and Village apply; when they do, Gram Panchayat cascades from District/Block, Village cascades from Gram Panchayat, and the Activity dropdown then filters to only the activities specific to that Village. Save now confirms and would be immediately visible on the Sahbhagita Activity Report.

As‑Is vs To‑Be — Abstract of RA Bill & Upload Activity

Side-by-side comparison of the current screen behaviour (captured across the original walkthrough and the 17-Jul-2026 UAT screenshots) against the redesigned target.

AS-IS (current)

  • District, Block, Gram Panchayat and Village dropdowns render empty (placeholder only) on both Upload Activity and Abstract of RA Bill (DEF-01, DEF-03).
  • Save on Upload Activity gives no confirmation and does not persist the record (DEF-02).
  • Category toggles Gram Panchayat/Village visibility only — the fields don't cascade from District/Block, and Activity is not narrowed to the selected Village (DEF-04).
  • Header fields (PIU, Scheme, ISA, Category, Activity, RA Bill) act only as query filters, not a printed report header.
  • Result table returns "No data available in table" for tested PIU/ISA/RA Bill combinations, even where Approved activities exist.
  • RA Bill Details drill-down only confirmed working under CP Manager login, opened by clicking anywhere on the row.

TO-BE (this change request)

  • District and Block always populated from live master data; Gram Panchayat populates from District/Block and Village populates from Gram Panchayat, whenever Category = Village Wise (DEF-01/DEF-03/DEF-04 fixed).
  • Save persists the record and confirms on-screen; the entry is immediately visible on the Sahbhagita Activity Report (DEF-02 fixed).
  • Once Village is selected, the Activity dropdown is filtered to activities specific to that Village instead of showing the full master list.
  • PIU, MVS, ISA selections render as a fixed report header, matching the target workbook layout.
  • RA Bill No. and Activity are multi-select, allowing more than one RA Bill / Activity to be chosen together (ENH-02, restoring As-Is multi-select intent).
  • Summary query fixed so any combination with Approved activities returns data (FR-09); the "No. of Activities" value itself is a hyperlink that opens the RA Bill Details drill-down (ENH-01), available identically on both ISA Manager and CP Manager logins (FR-13).
